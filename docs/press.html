Y-Bridge Games is an independent game studio in Ohio, founded in 2026. Its first game, Shardfall, is a real-time strategy game in development.

Shardfall

Genre
Real-time strategy, 2D isometric
Platforms
Windows, macOS, Linux
Status
In development, no release date yet

A fallen star shattered over the world of Kaelor and buried its power in the ground. Three alliances fight for the land above it. Players lead an army and its heroes through five ages, from tribes to starfaring empires, and win by taking their rival’s cities.

Logos

Transparent PNGs. Use the brass crest on dark backgrounds and the ivory one on light backgrounds.

Logo use

  • Write the name as Y-Bridge Games. The wordmark is always uppercase.
  • Keep the crest at least 96 px tall, with clear space of 15% of its height around it. Use the icon below that size.
  • Don’t stretch, recolor, outline or add effects to the logo, or put the brass crest on busy images.

Fonts: Oswald and Inter, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (Oswald, Inter).
