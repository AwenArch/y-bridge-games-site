About the studio

Y-Bridge Games is an independent game studio founded in 2026. Its motto, “Games built one bridge at a time,” describes how it works: small, tested steps toward finished games. Its first title, Shardfall, is a 2D isometric real-time strategy game currently in development.

Hero-led armies fight across the ages for territory. In the world of Kaelor, a fallen star shattered into shards of raw power called Aether. Players lead their commanders from shard-worshipping tribes to starfaring empires across five ages, where holding land means holding the Aether beneath it.

Using the logo

  • Write the studio name as Y-Bridge Games. The wordmark is always uppercase: Y-BRIDGE GAMES.
  • Leave clear space around the crest of at least 15% of its height on every side.
  • Show the crest at least 96 px tall. For anything smaller, use the icon.
  • Don’t stretch, rotate, outline, recolor, or add shadows or glows to the logo.
  • Don’t place the brass crest on busy or mid-tone images.
  • Don’t retype the wordmark in another font.
