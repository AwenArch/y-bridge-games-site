Shardfall

A fallen star shattered over the world of Kaelor and buried its power in the ground. Three alliances fight for the land above it: the Veiled Concord, who believe the star has a soul; the Forged Accord, who see fuel; and the Hunger, who were born from it.

Lead an army and its heroes through five ages, from tribes to starfaring empires. Borders decide where you can build, and the only way to win is to take your rival’s cities.

In development. No release date yet.
